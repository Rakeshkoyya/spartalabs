Who we are

Most software asks your business to change. We build it the other way round.

Sparta Labs is an IT solutions company that designs, builds and runs the systems businesses depend on.

Most businesses do not need more software. They need software that fits how they actually work. Most of what they are sold does the opposite, asking a school, a production house or a distributor to rearrange itself around a product built for someone else.

We start with how the work really happens, not how the org chart says it does, and we build the system to match. It is slower to begin and far faster to live with.

Every engagement gets a specialist for every layer of the system, and one named lead who owns the whole thing from the first call to long after launch.

Common questions

Straight answers before
you ask.

{{i:question}} Who owns the code we pay for?

You do. Full source handover, documented, deployed to your own infrastructure if you want it there. No licence to renew.

{{i:question}} How do you price a project?

Fixed scope when the requirement is settled, a dedicated pod when it will evolve, and a retainer for live systems. We recommend one after discovery.

{{i:question}} How long does it take?

A business website takes four to six weeks. A custom platform takes three to six months to first release, then keeps improving.

{{i:question}} Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, before the first detailed conversation. Send yours or use ours.

{{i:question}} Can you work with our existing team?

Yes. We can be the whole build team, a pod alongside your in-house team, or the people who take over a system someone else left behind.

{{i:question}} Do you take on existing systems?

Yes. We start with an audit that tells you plainly what to keep, what to replace and what it will cost.

{{i:question}} What happens after launch?

Monitoring, security patching and improvements under a support agreement with response times written into it.

{{i:question}} Is my business a good fit?

If your work does not fit neatly into off-the-shelf software, almost certainly. If a product you can buy solves it, we will tell you.
