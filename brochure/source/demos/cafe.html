MODULE 03For the owner

How the rewards engine works

You set the rules once in plain words. The system applies them to every bill and sends the right offer to the right guest on its own.

  1. 1
    Set how guests earnFor example 1 point for every ₹10, double points on slow weekday afternoons.
  2. 2
    Set tier levelsGuests move up as they spend more, and earn faster once they do.
  3. 3
    Create automatic campaigns"Not seen in 30 days" gets a comeback offer by app and WhatsApp.
  4. 4
    See what workedHow many came back, what they spent, and the cost of the rewards.

MODULE 06For the store manager

Inventory management

Know what is on your shelves without counting every day. Each item on the menu has a recipe, so every sale reduces the right ingredients.

  1. 1
    Stock at a glanceEvery ingredient, how much is left and how many days it will last.
  2. 2
    Recipes deduct stockSell one cappuccino and 18 g beans, 150 ml milk and one cup are taken off.
  3. 3
    Low stock alertsA warning before you run out, not after a guest is told "sorry".
  4. 4
    One-tap purchase ordersThe system suggests how much to order and sends it to your supplier.
  5. 5
    Expiry & wastageUse-first warnings for milk and bakes, and a log of what was thrown away.
Why it matters
Less wasteSpot theft & over-pouringNever run out
