MODULE 03For the owner

How the coupon engine works

You decide the rules once. The system hands out the right coupon at the right moment, stops misuse, and shows you if it made money.

  1. 1
    Build a coupon in a minuteFlat ₹, percentage, buy-2-get-1 or free gift. Set minimum bill and expiry.
  2. 2
    Guard your marginExclude sale items, one use per customer, only on new arrivals, only in store.
  3. 3
    Automatic triggersCoupons issue themselves: after a purchase, on app download, on birthdays, or when a customer goes quiet.
  4. 4
    See the returnCoupons issued, used, extra sales they brought and what the discounts cost.
